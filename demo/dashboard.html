How failover works here

The router health-checks all three backends and always routes to the healthiest one. Hit the chaos button: the victim is marked down, the monitor records the outage, and the router shifts traffic to the next healthy cloud within one check — exactly what the production Cloudflare Worker (orchestration/router/src/worker.js) does, but in milliseconds.
